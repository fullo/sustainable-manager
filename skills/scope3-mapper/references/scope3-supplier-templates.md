# Scope 3 Supplier Data Request Templates

Templates for requesting emissions data from suppliers, in English and Italian.

---

## Template A: Basic Supplier Questionnaire

**Purpose:** For small or non-expert suppliers who may not have formal carbon reporting in place.

---

### English Version

**Subject: Carbon Footprint Data Request — Supply Chain Emissions Reporting**

Dear [Supplier Name],

As part of our commitment to sustainability and in compliance with the EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD), we are measuring the carbon footprint of our full value chain. Your data is essential to help us accurately report Scope 3 emissions and identify opportunities for joint improvement.

Please complete the following questionnaire for your most recent reporting year:

**Section 1: General Information**
- Company name: ___
- Contact person for sustainability data: ___
- Reporting year: ___
- Main products/services supplied to us: ___
- Annual revenue from our business relationship (approximate): ___

**Section 2: Energy Consumption**
- Total electricity consumption (kWh/year): ___
- Electricity source (grid mix / renewable %): ___
- Total natural gas consumption (m3 or kWh/year): ___
- Total diesel/petrol consumption (litres/year): ___
- Other fuels (type and quantity): ___

**Section 3: Existing Carbon Reporting**
- Do you currently measure your carbon footprint? (Yes/No): ___
- If yes, what is your total annual CO2e (tonnes)? ___
- Scope 1 emissions (tCO2e): ___
- Scope 2 emissions (tCO2e): ___
- Methodology used (e.g., GHG Protocol, ISO 14064): ___
- Is the data externally verified? (Yes/No): ___

**Section 4: Environmental Certifications**
- ISO 14001 (Environmental Management): Yes / No / In progress
- ISO 50001 (Energy Management): Yes / No / In progress
- EcoVadis rating (if applicable): ___
- Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi) commitment: Yes / No
- Other certifications: ___

**Section 5: Product-Level Data (if available)**
- Carbon footprint per unit of product supplied to us (kgCO2e/unit): ___
- Methodology for product-level calculation: ___
- Environmental Product Declaration (EPD) available? (Yes/No): ___

**How your data will be used:**
Your data will be used solely to calculate our Scope 3 emissions as required by CSRD/ESRS E1 and to identify decarbonization opportunities across our value chain. Individual supplier data will be treated confidentially and aggregated in our public reporting.

**Deadline:** Please return this questionnaire by [date].
**Support:** If you need help completing this, contact [name] at [email].

Thank you for your collaboration in this important effort.

## Template C: CBAM-Specific Data Request

**Purpose:** For non-EU suppliers of goods covered by the Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM): cement, iron/steel, aluminium, fertilisers, electricity, hydrogen.

---

### English Version

**Subject: CBAM Embedded Emissions Data Request**

Dear [Supplier Name],

Under the EU Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism (CBAM), we are required to report the embedded emissions of certain imported goods. As a supplier of CBAM-covered products, we need the following data for each product supplied to us.

**Product Information**
- Product description: ___
- CN (Combined Nomenclature) code: ___
- CBAM product category: Cement / Iron & Steel / Aluminium / Fertiliser / Electricity / Hydrogen
- Quantity supplied in reporting period: ___ (tonnes or MWh)

**Embedded Emissions — Direct (Scope 1)**
- Direct emissions from production process (tCO2e per tonne of product): ___
- Production process description: ___
- Fuels used in production (type and quantity): ___
- Process emissions (non-combustion, e.g., calcination): ___

**Embedded Emissions — Indirect (Scope 2)**
- Electricity consumed in production (MWh per tonne of product): ___
- Electricity source: Grid / On-site generation / PPA / Renewable certificates
- Grid emission factor of production location (tCO2e/MWh): ___
- Country and region of production: ___

**Carbon Price**
- Is a carbon price or tax applied in your jurisdiction? (Yes/No): ___
- If yes: Carbon pricing mechanism (ETS / tax / other): ___
- Carbon price paid (EUR/tCO2e or local currency equivalent): ___
- Free allocation received (if ETS): ___
- Effective carbon cost per tonne of product: ___

**Verification**
- Are the above emissions data verified by an accredited verifier? (Yes/No): ___
- Verifier name and accreditation: ___
- Verification standard: ___

**Important:** During the CBAM transitional period, default values may be used where actual data is unavailable, but actual installation-level data is strongly preferred and will be required in the definitive period.

## Cover Letter Template

---

### English Version

**Subject: Partnership for Value Chain Decarbonization — Data Request**

Dear [Name],

I hope this message finds you well.

As you may be aware, the EU Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD) requires companies to measure and report greenhouse gas emissions across their entire value chain (Scope 3). This means that we need to work closely with our supply chain partners to gather accurate emissions data.

**Why we are reaching out:**
- Regulatory requirement: CSRD mandates Scope 3 disclosure from [reporting year]
- Mutual benefit: understanding our shared carbon footprint helps both organizations identify efficiency opportunities and cost savings
- Market advantage: customers and investors increasingly require supply chain transparency

**What we are asking:**
We have enclosed a questionnaire requesting basic energy and emissions data. We estimate it will take approximately [30 minutes / 1 hour] to complete.

**How we will support you:**
- We are available for a call to walk through the questionnaire together
- We can share guidance on carbon measurement for organizations new to the topic
- Aggregated data and insights from this exercise will be shared back with you
- We are exploring options for joint capacity-building programs

**Timeline:**
- Questionnaire deadline: [date]
- Follow-up call (if needed): [date range]
- Results shared back: [date]

**Confidentiality:**
Your individual data will be treated as strictly confidential. Only aggregated, anonymized data will appear in our public sustainability report.

We see this as the beginning of a collaborative journey toward a lower-carbon value chain. Thank you for your partnership.

Best regards,
[Name, Title]
[Company]
[Contact details]
