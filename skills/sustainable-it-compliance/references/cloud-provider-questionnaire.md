# Cloud Provider Sustainability Questionnaire

Question module for cloud and hosting providers — the typical blind spot of ICT Scope 3. Use standalone or as an additional module of the supplier-engagement skill questionnaires. Questions in English with Italian version (EN / IT). Last verified: July 2026.

Scoring guidance: answers backed by published, region-level, third-party-verified data score high; global averages and unverified marketing claims score low.

---

## Section 1 — Region-Level Efficiency Data

1. Do you publish **PUE per region/data centre** (not just a global average)?
   *Pubblicate il PUE per regione/data centre (non solo la media globale)?*
2. Do you publish **WUE per region**, and do you flag water-stressed locations?
   *Pubblicate il WUE per regione, segnalando le localita in stress idrico?*
3. What is the **carbon-free energy (CFE) percentage per region** on an hourly basis, if available?
   *Qual e la percentuale di energia carbon-free per regione, se disponibile su base oraria?*
4. Do you contribute region metadata to the GSF **Real Time Cloud** dataset or equivalent?
   *Contribuite i metadati di regione al dataset Real Time Cloud della GSF o equivalente?*

## Section 2 — Renewable Energy Claims

5. What instruments back your renewable claims per region: PPAs, unbundled GOs/RECs, on-site generation? In what proportions?
   *Quali strumenti sostengono i claim rinnovabili per regione: PPA, GO/REC unbundled, generazione on-site? In che proporzioni?*
6. Are claims **market-based per GHG Protocol Scope 2 Guidance**, and do you also disclose location-based figures?
   *I claim sono market-based secondo la GHG Protocol Scope 2 Guidance, e pubblicate anche i dati location-based?*

## Section 3 — Customer Carbon Reporting

7. Do you provide a **customer-level carbon dashboard/API**? Which scopes does it cover (your Scope 1+2 allocated, your upstream Scope 3, embodied hardware)?
   *Fornite dashboard/API di carbonio a livello cliente? Quali scope copre (vostri Scope 1+2 allocati, vostro Scope 3 upstream, embodied hardware)?*
8. What is the **allocation methodology** (by usage metric, by spend), and is it third-party verified?
   *Qual e la metodologia di allocazione (per metrica d'uso, per spesa), ed e verificata da terza parte?*
9. What is the **data latency and granularity** (monthly/daily, per service, per region)?
   *Qual e la latenza e granularita dei dati (mensile/giornaliera, per servizio, per regione)?*

## Section 4 — Commitments and Compliance

10. Do you have **SBTi-validated targets**? Which version/scope?
    *Avete target validati SBTi? Quale versione/scope?*
11. Are your EU data centres reported under **EED Art. 12**, and do you share the relevant KPIs with colocation/cloud customers on request?
    *I vostri data centre EU sono riportati sotto EED art. 12, e condividete i KPI rilevanti con i clienti su richiesta?*
12. Do you adhere to the **EU Code of Conduct for Data Centre Energy Efficiency** / Climate Neutral Data Centre Pact?
    *Aderite al Code of Conduct EU per l'efficienza dei data centre / Climate Neutral Data Centre Pact?*
13. What is your hardware **lifetime extension and reuse policy** (servers refurbished/resold vs scrapped)?
    *Qual e la vostra policy di estensione vita e riuso dell'hardware (server rigenerati/rivenduti vs rottamati)?*

## Section 5 — Workload Placement Support

14. Do you offer tools for **carbon-aware workload placement/scheduling** (low-carbon regions, time-shifting)?
    *Offrite strumenti per il placement/scheduling carbon-aware dei workload (regioni low-carbon, time-shifting)?*
15. Are there contractual or latency constraints that prevent placing our workloads in your lowest-carbon EU regions?
    *Ci sono vincoli contrattuali o di latenza che impediscono di collocare i nostri workload nelle vostre regioni EU a minor intensita carbonica?*

---

## Red Flags

- Only global PUE averages, no regional data → weak basis for Scope 3
- Renewable claims backed only by unbundled RECs with no location-based disclosure
- Carbon dashboard covering only the provider's Scope 2 allocation, omitting embodied hardware
- "Carbon neutral" service claims based on offsets → EmpCo exposure for YOUR downstream claims too
