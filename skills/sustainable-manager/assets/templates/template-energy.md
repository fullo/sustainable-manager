# ESRS Report Template — Energy / Utilities Sector

**Settore / Sector:** Energy and Utilities (power generation, gas distribution, renewable energy, multi-utilities)
**Standard di riferimento / Reference standard:** ESRS (European Sustainability Reporting Standards) — Delegated Regulation (EU) 2023/2772
**Template version:** 1.0 — April 2026

---

> **How to use this template / Come usare questo modello**
>
> 1. Replace all `[...]` placeholders with your company's actual data.
> 2. Delete guidance notes (blockquotes starting with ">") before publishing.
> 3. All metrics should refer to the reporting period unless stated otherwise.
> 4. Where a disclosure is not material, document the reasoning in the materiality assessment section.
> 5. This template includes energy-sector-specific metrics (generation mix, coal phase-out, grid emissions, just transition).
