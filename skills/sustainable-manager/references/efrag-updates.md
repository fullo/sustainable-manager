## ESRS Simplification

In response to the EU Omnibus I Proposal, EFRAG submitted its technical advice on simplified ESRS to the European Commission on December 3, 2025.

### Headline Changes
- **61% reduction in mandatory datapoints** across all 12 standards
- **All voluntary datapoints removed** — if it's in the standard, it's mandatory
- **Top-down materiality assessment** now permitted (no exhaustive bottom-up analysis required)
- **Value chain data relief** — companies not required to perform exhaustive searches; sector/regional averages acceptable
- **Wider use of estimates** without "undue cost or effort"
- **Fair presentation principle** replaces extensive disclosure checklists

### Why It Happened
The original ESRS (adopted 2023) faced criticism for complexity, especially from:
- SMEs and mid-cap companies entering CSRD for the first time
- Industry associations citing disproportionate reporting burden
- Member states pushing for competitiveness simplification

The Omnibus I Proposal (February 2025) mandated simplification while maintaining the core double materiality framework.

## Amended ESRS — Key Changes by Standard

### ESRS 1 & 2 (Cross-cutting)
- Materiality is now the primary filter — non-material topics omitted entirely
- Top-down materiality approach permitted
- Expanded flexibility in geographic aggregation
- Human rights policies moved to ESRS 2 as cross-cutting (not repeated per topical standard)
- General Disclosure Requirements (policies, actions, metrics, targets) consolidated in ESRS 2

### ESRS E1 (Climate Change)
- **Transition plan**: No longer mandatory, but must disclose if absent ("comply or explain")
- **Scenario analysis**: Shifted to voluntary
- Explicit GHG Protocol Scope 2/3 boundary alignment
- Phase-ins for anticipated financial effects through 2029
- Core GHG metrics (Scope 1/2/3) remain mandatory when climate is material

### ESRS E2 (Pollution)
- Microplastics (primary and secondary) now explicit mandatory disclosures
- Substances of concern limited to SVHC for non-chemical firms
- Chemical undertakings have broader substance reporting with phase-ins
- Uses European Pollutant Release Transfer Register (E-PRTR) thresholds

### ESRS E3 (Water)
- Water intensity metric **removed**
- Focus sharpened on **water-stressed locations**
- Core metrics simplified: withdrawal, discharge, consumption, reuse, storage

### ESRS E4 (Biodiversity)
- Transition plan required only if already publicly disclosed
- New **"area of influence"** concept with buffer distance definitions
- Consolidated location-based biodiversity metrics
- Simplified for companies with low biodiversity exposure

### ESRS E5 (Resource Use & Circular Economy)
- **"Key materials"** introduced as defined concept (replaces biological/technical categories)
- Secondary materials disclosure clarified
- New metric: **waste with unknown final destination**
- New metric: **designed recyclability rate**

### ESRS S1 (Own Workforce)
- Adequate wages anchored to **ILO living wage principles**
- **Unadjusted gender pay gap** remains mandatory
- Adjusted gap becomes entity-specific supplementary
- Workforce metrics use revised thresholds (10 largest countries, 50-employee de minimis)

### ESRS S2 (Value Chain Workers)
- Policies/actions narrowed to **material upstream/downstream** impacts only
- Grievance mechanisms merged with engagement requirements
- Fewer datapoints overall — focused on due diligence outcomes

### ESRS S3 (Affected Communities)
- Incident definition narrowed to **substantiated, internationally-recognized rights** cases
- Aggregation by type/geography permitted
- **FPIC** (Free, Prior, Informed Consent) explicit for Indigenous peoples

### ESRS S4 (Consumers & End-users)
- Incident reporting retained where material
- Narrative requirements simplified

### ESRS G1 (Business Conduct)
- Corruption/bribery metrics: scope clarified
- Late payment metric **removed for SMEs**
- Political influence/lobbying simplified
- Leaner overall structure

## Sector Standards

### Status: Cancelled
The Omnibus I Proposal **cancels the rollout of sector-specific ESRS**. EFRAG has dropped them from its roadmap.

Instead:
- Core standards will be made **flexible enough to apply across industries**
- Existing sector-specific content in GRI Sector Standards and SASB industry standards can be used as supplementary reference
- EFRAG's research on Financial Institutions and Road Transport standards may inform future guidance but will not become mandatory standards

### What This Means for Reporting
- Companies should NOT wait for sector standards — report under the core ESRS
- Use GRI Sector Standards for sector-specific material topic identification
- Use SASB industry standards for investor-focused metrics
- The flexibility of ESRS entity-specific disclosures allows sector adaptation

## SME Standards

### VSME (Voluntary SME Standard)
- European Commission issued a **recommendation** (not mandatory)
- Designed for non-listed SMEs that want to report voluntarily
- Simplified structure with fewer datapoints
- Digital template and XBRL taxonomy under development (EFRAG research phase)

### LSME (Listed SME Standard)
- Listed SMEs fall under CSRD Wave 3 (FY 2028)
- Will use the amended ESRS with proportionality provisions
- Additional transitional relief may apply

## Implementation Resources

### EFRAG Resources
- **ESRS Knowledge Hub** (launched Dec 2025): Central platform for navigating ESRS
- **Implementation Guidance (IG)**: Series of guidance documents
  - IG 1: Materiality assessment
  - IG 2: Value chain
  - IG 3: List of datapoints (revision expected Q1 2026)
- **ESRS Q&A Platform**: Technical questions and clarifications

### Key External Resources
- **European Commission CSRD page**: Legal texts, delegated acts
- **GHG Protocol**: Scope 1/2/3 calculation standards
- **SBTi**: Target-setting criteria and validation
- **GRI-ESRS Interoperability Index**: Mapping between frameworks
- **ISSB-ESRS Interoperability Guidance**: For companies reporting under both

### For Italian Companies
- **OIC (Organismo Italiano di Contabilità)**: Italian accounting body, CSRD transposition guidance
- **Consob**: Supervisory guidance for listed companies
- **Confindustria**: Industry association support and toolkits
- **CNDCEC**: Professional guidance for auditors on sustainability assurance
