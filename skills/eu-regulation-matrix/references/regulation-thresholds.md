# EU Sustainability Regulation Thresholds — Comprehensive Reference

Last updated: April 2026

---

## 1. CSRD / ESRS (Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive)

**Legal basis**: Directive 2022/2464/EU amending Directive 2013/34/EU, as modified by the Omnibus Simplification Package (February 2026)

### Original Scope (pre-Omnibus)

Companies meeting **2 of 3 criteria**:

| Criterion | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| Employees | 250+ |
| Net turnover | EUR 40M+ |
| Total assets | EUR 20M+ |

### Post-Omnibus Scope (February 2026)

Significantly narrowed. Companies must meet **both criteria**:

| Criterion | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| Employees | 1,000+ |
| Net turnover | EUR 450M+ |

This change removes an estimated 80% of companies from the original scope.

### Listed SMEs

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Scope | Companies listed on EU regulated markets that do not meet large-company thresholds |
| Applicable from | FY2026 (reporting in 2027) |
| Opt-out | Possible until FY2028, with brief justification in management report |
| Standard | ESRS LSME (Listed SME set) |

### Non-EU Companies

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Threshold | EUR 150M+ net turnover generated in the EU |
| Condition | Must have at least one EU subsidiary or branch exceeding EUR 40M turnover |
| Applicable from | FY2028 (reporting in 2029) |
| Standard | Separate non-EU ESRS set |

### Phase-in Timeline

| Phase | Applicable from | Companies |
|-------|----------------|-----------|
| Wave 1 | FY2024 (report in 2025) | Large PIEs already under NFRD (500+ employees) |
| Wave 2 | FY2025 (report in 2026) | Other large companies (original 2-of-3 criteria) |
| Wave 3 | FY2026 (report in 2027) | Listed SMEs |
| Wave 4 | FY2028 (report in 2029) | Non-EU companies with EUR 150M+ EU revenue |
| Omnibus amended ESRS | FY2027 (report in 2028) | Revised standards applying to remaining in-scope companies |

**Note**: The Omnibus package effectively means Wave 2 companies that fall below the new 1000-employee / EUR 450M thresholds are released from CSRD obligations for FY2027 onwards. They may still need to report for FY2025 and FY2026 under the original thresholds.

### Value Chain Data Cap

- Entities with fewer than **1,000 employees** may refuse data requests from value chain partners for ESRS reporting purposes
- This was introduced by the Omnibus package to relieve burden on SMEs

### Key ESRS Standards

| Standard | Topic |
|----------|-------|
| ESRS 1 | General requirements |
| ESRS 2 | General disclosures (mandatory for all) |
| ESRS E1-E5 | Environmental (climate, pollution, water, biodiversity, circular economy) |
| ESRS S1-S4 | Social (own workforce, value chain workers, communities, consumers) |
| ESRS G1 | Governance (business conduct) |

Materiality assessment determines which topical standards (E1-E5, S1-S4, G1) are reportable, except ESRS 2 which is always mandatory.

## 2. CSDDD (Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive)

**Legal basis**: Directive 2024/1760/EU, as amended by the Omnibus Simplification Package

### Thresholds (Post-Omnibus)

| Criterion | Threshold |
|-----------|-----------|
| Employees | 5,000+ |
| Worldwide net turnover | EUR 1.5B+ |

Both criteria must be met. Originally the directive targeted companies with 500+ employees and EUR 150M+ turnover — the Omnibus raised these thresholds by approximately 10x.

### Key Timeline

| Milestone | Date |
|-----------|------|
| Directive entered into force | July 2024 |
| Original transposition deadline | July 2026 |
| Revised transposition deadline (Omnibus) | Mid-2029 |
| First compliance obligations | 2029 (largest companies) |

### Scope Details

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Due diligence scope | Human rights and environmental impacts across the value chain |
| Climate transition plan | Requirement REMOVED by Omnibus |
| Civil liability | Maintained but narrowed — burden of proof adjustments |
| Sector-specific | No sector-specific thresholds (uniform application) |
| Non-EU companies | EUR 1.5B+ net turnover generated in the EU |

### Due Diligence Obligations

1. Integrate due diligence into company policies
2. Identify and assess actual/potential adverse impacts
3. Prevent, mitigate, or bring to an end adverse impacts
4. Establish and maintain a complaints procedure
5. Monitor effectiveness of due diligence measures
6. Publicly communicate on due diligence

## 3. CBAM (Carbon Border Adjustment Mechanism)

**Legal basis**: Regulation (EU) 2023/956

### Covered Products

| Product Category | CN Codes (key examples) |
|-----------------|------------------------|
| Cement | 2523 10, 2523 21, 2523 29, 2523 30, 2523 90 |
| Iron and steel | 72xx (various), 7301-7311, 7318, 7326 |
| Aluminium | 7601, 7603-7614, 7616 |
| Fertilizers | 2808 00, 2814, 3102, 3105 |
| Hydrogen | 2804 10 00 |
| Electricity | 2716 00 00 |

### De Minimis Threshold

| Detail | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Threshold | 50 tonnes per product category per year |
| Effect | Importers below this threshold are exempt from CBAM obligations |
| Impact | Removes approximately 90% of transitional period reporters |
| Applies from | Definitive phase (January 2026) |

### Timeline

| Phase | Period | Obligations |
|-------|--------|-------------|
| Transitional period | 1 Oct 2023 – 31 Dec 2025 | Quarterly reporting of embedded emissions (no financial obligation) |
| Definitive phase begins | 1 Jan 2026 | Must be authorized CBAM declarant; annual declarations |
| First CBAM certificates purchase | 2026 | Purchase certificates corresponding to embedded emissions |
| First certificate surrender | 30 Sept 2027 | Surrender certificates for 2026 imports |

### Emission Calculation

| Product Category | Direct Emissions | Indirect Emissions |
|-----------------|-----------------|-------------------|
| Cement | Yes | Yes |
| Iron and steel | Yes | No (for most products) |
| Aluminium | Yes | Yes |
| Fertilizers | Yes | Yes |
| Hydrogen | Yes | Yes |
| Electricity | Yes (by definition) | N/A |

### Key Obligations for Importers

1. Register as **authorized CBAM declarant** with competent authority
2. Calculate **embedded emissions** using actual data or default values
3. Purchase **CBAM certificates** at EU ETS auction price
4. Submit **annual CBAM declaration** by 31 May each year
5. Surrender certificates by **30 September** each year
6. Deduct carbon price already paid in country of origin

## 4. EU Taxonomy (Regulation (EU) 2020/852)

### Applicability

| Who | Condition |
|-----|-----------|
| Companies subject to CSRD | Art. 8 disclosure obligations apply automatically |
| Financial market participants | Required to disclose Taxonomy alignment of financial products |
| EU/Member States | When setting public measures or standards for green bonds |

### Six Environmental Objectives

| # | Objective |
|---|-----------|
| 1 | Climate change mitigation |
| 2 | Climate change adaptation |
| 3 | Sustainable use and protection of water and marine resources |
| 4 | Transition to a circular economy |
| 5 | Pollution prevention and control |
| 6 | Protection and restoration of biodiversity and ecosystems |

### Three-Part Alignment Test

For an economic activity to be Taxonomy-aligned:

1. **Substantial contribution** to at least one environmental objective (meeting technical screening criteria)
2. **Do No Significant Harm (DNSH)** to the other five objectives
3. **Minimum safeguards** compliance (OECD Guidelines, UN Guiding Principles, ILO core conventions)

### Art. 8 Disclosure Metrics

| Metric | Description |
|--------|-------------|
| Revenue (turnover) | % of net revenue from Taxonomy-aligned activities |
| CapEx | % of capital expenditure related to Taxonomy-aligned activities or CapEx plans |
| OpEx | % of operating expenditure related to Taxonomy-aligned activities |

Companies must disclose these as KPIs in their management report, broken down by environmental objective and showing eligible vs. aligned percentages.

### Delegated Acts

| Delegated Act | Coverage |
|--------------|---------|
| Climate DA (2021/2139) | Technical screening criteria for objectives 1 and 2 |
| Environmental DA (2023/2486) | Technical screening criteria for objectives 3-6 |
| Complementary DA (2022/1214) | Nuclear and gas activities under specific conditions |
| Art. 8 DA (2021/2178) | Disclosure methodology and templates |

## 5. PPWR (Packaging and Packaging Waste Regulation)

**Legal basis**: Regulation (EU) 2025/... (replacing Directive 94/62/EC)

### Scope

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Who is covered | All producers, importers, distributors, and fillers of packaging placed on the EU market |
| What is covered | All packaging regardless of material (plastic, paper, glass, metal, wood, composite) |
| Entry into force | August 2026 |

### Key Targets and Deadlines

| Target | Deadline | Detail |
|--------|----------|--------|
| Recyclability requirements | 2030 | All packaging must be designed for recycling |
| Recycled content — plastic (contact-sensitive) | 2030 | 10% minimum |
| Recycled content — plastic (contact-sensitive) | 2040 | 50% minimum |
| Recycled content — plastic (other) | 2030 | 35% minimum |
| Recycled content — plastic (other) | 2040 | 65% minimum |
| Packaging waste reduction | 2030 | 5% reduction vs. 2018 |
| Packaging waste reduction | 2035 | 10% reduction vs. 2018 |
| Packaging waste reduction | 2040 | 15% reduction vs. 2018 |
| Reuse targets — beverages | 2030 | Varies by beverage type (10-25%) |
| Reuse targets — transport packaging | 2030 | 30-90% depending on type |
| Empty space ratio | 2030 | Max 50% empty space in grouped and transport packaging |
| DRS (Deposit Return System) | 2029 | Mandatory for single-use plastic and metal beverage containers |

### Key Obligations

1. **Extended Producer Responsibility (EPR)**: financial and organizational responsibility for end-of-life management
2. **Labelling**: harmonized EU labelling for material identification and sorting instructions
3. **Minimization**: packaging must be minimized in weight and volume while maintaining functionality
4. **Substances of concern**: restrictions on PFAS and other hazardous substances in food-contact packaging

## 6. EUDR (EU Deforestation Regulation)

**Legal basis**: Regulation (EU) 2023/1115

### Covered Commodities

| # | Commodity | Example Derived Products |
|---|-----------|------------------------|
| 1 | Soy | Soybean oil, soy meal, soy lecithin, tofu |
| 2 | Palm oil | Palm kernel oil, oleochemicals, biodiesel feedstock |
| 3 | Cocoa | Chocolate, cocoa butter, cocoa powder |
| 4 | Coffee | Roasted coffee, instant coffee, coffee extracts |
| 5 | Rubber | Natural rubber, tyres, latex products |
| 6 | Wood | Timber, paper, pulp, printed products, furniture, charcoal |
| 7 | Cattle | Beef, leather, tallow, gelatin |

### Due Diligence Requirements

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Deforestation-free | Products must not originate from land deforested after 31 December 2020 |
| Legal compliance | Must be produced in compliance with the laws of the country of origin |
| Traceability | Geolocation data (coordinates or polygons) of all plots of land of production required |
| Due diligence statement | Mandatory statement submitted via EU information system before placing on market or exporting |

### Risk Benchmarking

| Risk Category | Due Diligence Level | Checks Frequency |
|--------------|--------------------|-|
| Low risk countries | Simplified due diligence | Reduced |
| Standard risk countries | Standard due diligence | Standard |
| High risk countries | Enhanced due diligence | Increased inspections (9% of operators) |

### Timeline

| Milestone | Date |
|-----------|------|
| Entry into force | 29 June 2023 |
| Application — large operators | 30 December 2025 (delayed from original June 2025) |
| Application — SMEs | 30 June 2026 |
| Country benchmarking list publication | Expected H2 2025 |

### Operator Size Categories

| Category | Threshold | Simplified DD available? |
|----------|-----------|------------------------|
| Large operators | Default | No — full due diligence required |
| SME operators | Meets EU SME definition | Yes, for low-risk country sourcing only |

## 7. SFDR (Sustainable Finance Disclosure Regulation)

**Legal basis**: Regulation (EU) 2019/2088, supplemented by Delegated Regulation (EU) 2022/1288

### Scope

| Who | Examples |
|-----|---------|
| Financial market participants (FMPs) | Asset managers (UCITS, AIFM), insurance companies offering IBIPs, pension fund providers, portfolio managers, venture capital/PE fund managers |
| Financial advisers | Investment firms providing advice, insurance intermediaries, credit institutions providing advice |

### Disclosure Levels

| Level | Requirement |
|-------|-------------|
| Entity-level | Policies on sustainability risk integration; PAI consideration (comply or explain for FMPs with <500 employees, mandatory for 500+) |
| Product-level | Pre-contractual, website, and periodic disclosures for each financial product |

### Product Classification

| Article | Description | Key Requirements |
|---------|-------------|-----------------|
| Art. 6 | Products without sustainability characteristics | Basic sustainability risk disclosure |
| Art. 8 | Products promoting environmental or social characteristics ("light green") | Disclose how E/S characteristics are met; binding elements in investment strategy |
| Art. 9 | Products with sustainable investment as objective ("dark green") | Disclose sustainable investment objective; demonstrate all investments contribute |

### Principal Adverse Impact (PAI) Indicators

| Category | Number of Mandatory Indicators |
|----------|-------------------------------|
| Climate and environment | 9 mandatory indicators |
| Social and employee matters | 5 mandatory indicators |
| Additional opt-in indicators | 46 indicators (must select at least 1 environment + 1 social) |

Key mandatory PAI indicators include:
- GHG emissions (Scope 1, 2, 3)
- Carbon footprint
- GHG intensity
- Fossil fuel exposure
- Non-renewable energy share
- Energy consumption intensity
- Biodiversity impact
- Water emissions
- Hazardous waste
- UNGC/OECD violations
- Gender pay gap
- Board gender diversity
- Controversial weapons exposure

### Ongoing Review

The European Commission has published a comprehensive review of SFDR (September 2023). Potential revisions could include:
- Replacing Art. 8/9 with a formal product categorization system
- Simplifying PAI disclosure
- Aligning better with EU Taxonomy
- Expected Level 1 revision proposal: 2025-2026

## 8. Italian Regulatory Specifics

### CSRD Transposition in Italy

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Transposition instrument | D.Lgs. attuativo della Direttiva 2022/2464/UE |
| Supervisory authority (listed entities) | **Consob** (Commissione Nazionale per le Societa e la Borsa) |
| Supervisory authority (banks/insurance) | **Banca d'Italia** / **IVASS** |
| Accounting body | **OIC** (Organismo Italiano di Contabilita) |
| Pre-existing law | **D.Lgs. 254/2016** (DNF — Dichiarazione Non Finanziaria) |

### D.Lgs. 254/2016 — Residual Applicability

| Status | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Current status | Remains in force for entities not yet transitioned to CSRD/ESRS |
| Applicable to | Enti di interesse pubblico (EIP) with 500+ employees and EUR 40M+ turnover or EUR 20M+ total assets |
| Reporting standard | GRI Standards (most common) or other recognized frameworks |
| Phase-out | Entities in CSRD Wave 1 (FY2024) report under ESRS; D.Lgs. 254 becomes residual for remaining EIPs |
| Full sunset | Expected when all in-scope entities transition to CSRD/ESRS |

### Consob Deliberations

| Delibera | Subject |
|----------|---------|
| Delibera n. 20267 (2018) | Original implementation of D.Lgs. 254/2016 reporting |
| Subsequent Consob communications | CSRD transposition guidance, ESRS adoption instructions |
| XBRL taxonomy | Consob alignment with EFRAG digital taxonomy for ESRS reporting |

### CBAM in Italy

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Competent authority | **Agenzia delle Dogane e dei Monopoli** (ADM) |
| Declarant registration | Through ADM portal, linked to EU CBAM registry |
| Support | ADM circulars on CBAM transitional reporting and definitive obligations |
| Customs integration | CBAM declarations integrated with existing customs procedures (AIDA system) |

### EUDR in Italy

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Competent authority | **MASE** (Ministero dell'Ambiente e della Sicurezza Energetica) |
| Enforcement | Coordination with Guardia di Finanza and Agenzia delle Dogane |
| Key sectors impacted | Furniture/wood (Distretto del Mobile), leather/cattle, food/agri (coffee, cocoa imports) |

### PPWR in Italy

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Current framework | D.Lgs. 152/2006 Part IV (Testo Unico Ambientale) and D.Lgs. 116/2020 |
| EPR system | **CONAI** (Consorzio Nazionale Imballaggi) and material-specific consortia |
| Transition | PPWR as a Regulation will apply directly; national law adaptation required for enforcement |
| DRS implementation | Italy must establish deposit return system by 2029 |

### Italian Timeline Summary

| Year | Key Events for Italian Companies |
|------|--------------------------------|
| 2024 | FY2024 reporting: Wave 1 entities (large EIPs) begin CSRD/ESRS reporting |
| 2025 | CBAM transitional period ends (Dec); EUDR applicable to large operators (Dec); FY2025 reporting for Wave 2 entities under original thresholds |
| 2026 | CBAM definitive phase begins (Jan); Listed SMEs begin CSRD reporting (FY2026); PPWR enters into force (Aug); EUDR applicable to SMEs (Jun); Omnibus amendments take effect |
| 2027 | First CBAM certificate surrender (Sept); FY2027 reporting under amended ESRS |
| 2028 | Non-EU companies with EUR 150M+ EU revenue begin CSRD reporting (FY2028) |
| 2029 | CSDDD transposition deadline (mid-2029); DRS mandatory implementation |

## Cross-Regulation Interaction Map

| If subject to... | Then also consider... |
|------------------|----------------------|
| CSRD | EU Taxonomy Art. 8 (automatic), SFDR (if financial entity) |
| CSDDD | CSRD (likely applies if CSDDD thresholds met), EUDR (if relevant commodities in value chain) |
| CBAM | EU ETS (interaction with free allowance phase-out), CSRD Scope 1/2/3 reporting |
| EU Taxonomy | CSRD (prerequisite), SFDR (for financial products referencing Taxonomy) |
| SFDR | EU Taxonomy (for Art. 8/9 products), CSRD (PAI data sourced from investee ESRS reports) |
| PPWR | CSRD (circular economy disclosures under ESRS E5), EU Taxonomy (Objective 4) |
| EUDR | CSDDD (deforestation as environmental impact in due diligence), CSRD (biodiversity disclosures ESRS E4) |

## Quick Decision Matrix

Use this table for rapid initial assessment:

| Regulation | Size Gate | Sector Gate | Geography Gate |
|-----------|----------|-------------|---------------|
| CSRD | 1000+ empl. AND EUR 450M+ rev (or listed SME, or non-EU EUR 150M+) | All sectors | EU entities (or non-EU with EU presence) |
| CSDDD | 5000+ empl. AND EUR 1.5B+ rev | All sectors | EU entities (or non-EU with EUR 1.5B+ EU rev) |
| CBAM | No size gate (de minimis: 50t/year per product) | Importers of specific products | Imports from non-EU countries |
| EU Taxonomy | Follows CSRD scope | All sectors (activity-specific screening) | EU entities under CSRD |
| PPWR | No size gate | Packaging producers/importers/fillers | EU market |
| EUDR | No size gate (SME simplified DD) | 7 commodity supply chains | EU market placement or export |
| SFDR | No size gate | Financial sector only | EU financial market participants and advisers |
