Regulatory Coverage

Every reference verified against primary sources — regulatory state as of July 2026

CSRD & revised ESRS

Post-Omnibus thresholds (Directive (EU) 2026/470, in force 18 March 2026): 1,000+ employees and €450M+ turnover. Revised ESRS adopted 3 July 2026 — 61% fewer mandatory datapoints, applicable FY2027.

Directive (EU) 2026/470 · ESRS 2026

CBAM definitive phase

Cumulative 50t de minimis, certificate sales from February 2027, annual declaration due 30 September, 50% quarterly holding — the 2025 Omnibus changes most sources still get wrong.

Reg. (EU) 2025/2083

EU Taxonomy simplification

10% materiality threshold, simplified templates (−64% fields), optional two-year deferral for financial undertakings.

Delegated Reg. (EU) 2026/73

SBTi Net-Zero v2.0

Released June 2026: company categorization, separate Scope 1/2 targets, mandatory transition planning and assurance. Dual validation track until v2.0 becomes mandatory on 1 February 2028.

SBTi CNZS v2.0

Nature & biodiversity

TNFD LEAP with 733 adopting organisations, SBTN targets, revised ESRS E4, the Italian UNI/PdR 179:2025 framework with mandatory MSA, and the ISSB nature exposure draft expected at COP17.

TNFD · SBTN · UNI/PdR 179:2025

Sustainable IT

EED Art. 12 data centre reporting (500 kW threshold, PUE/WUE/ERF/REF), AI Act GPAI energy documentation, SCI for AI (ratified Dec 2025), Right to Repair, F-gas, European Accessibility Act.

EED 2023/1791 · AI Act · GSF standards

Value chain rules

CSDDD final thresholds and dates (application from July 2029), EUDR from December 2026, PPWR phased application, VSME value-chain cap protecting sub-1,000-employee suppliers.

CSDDD · EUDR · PPWR · VSME

Anti-greenwashing

Empowering Consumers Directive bans generic claims and offset-based neutrality claims from 27 September 2026 — with claim auditing and compliant rewording built into the skills.

Dir. (EU) 2024/825 (EmpCo)

How It Works

Consulting flows, not chatbots: every skill follows a structured method

1

Ask in Plain Language

"We import steel from Turkey, what do we need to do?" The right skill activates automatically — or invoke it with a slash command. Works in English and Italian.

→
2

Answer One Question at a Time

No data dump required. The skill guides you progressively; "I don't know" is a valid answer — it tells you how to estimate or where to find the data.

→
3

Get Reviewable Drafts

Applicability matrices, gap analyses, calculations with stated assumptions, charts, and policy drafts — working documents to review, never certifications.

Why Use It

Regulatory Precision

Every skill distinguishes in-force / adopted-not-yet-applicable / draft. Dates and thresholds verified against primary sources (EUR-Lex, EFRAG, Commission) as of July 2026.

Science-Based

Grounded in LCA methodology, planetary boundaries, and science-based targets — not in marketing narratives. Greenwashing detection is a first-class feature.

Tools Included

Python calculators (SCI, Scope 3, circularity, supplier scoring, charts), compilable checklists, bilingual questionnaires, benchmark datasets.

Italian Context Built In

Write in Italian and get the Italian regulatory layer: Consob, ADM, MASE, CONAI, CAM ICT, RAEE, D.Lgs. 125/2024, UNI/PdR 179:2025.

Book-Aligned

The sustainable IT skill mirrors the method of "Sustainable IT the Right Way": maturity first, then obligations, measurement, and governance.

Honest Outputs

Estimates are flagged as estimates, uncertainty ranges are stated, and every skill reminds you where legal counsel or an auditor must take over.

Installation

Option 1 — Claude Marketplace

claude plugin install sustainable-manager@fullo-plugins

Option 2 — Clone & Add

git clone https://github.com/fullo/sustainable-manager.git
claude plugin add /path/to/sustainable-manager

Requirements

Claude Code Python 3.8+ matplotlib / numpy

Python is needed only for the calculators and charts — Claude runs the scripts for you. Update with claude plugin update sustainable-manager@fullo-plugins.
